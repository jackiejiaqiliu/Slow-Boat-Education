Traditional Text Series

Dr. Wang’s Lectures on Jin Gui Yao Lue (Essential from the Golden Cabinet)

Dr. Sanhu Wang

A systematic study of the Jin Gui Yao Lue (Essential from the Golden Cabinet) with emphasis on modern clinical importance. Often considered to proceed Shang Han Lun (Treatise on Cold Injury), Zhang Zhong Jing’s Jin Gui Yao Lue provides methodologies in the study, diagnosis, and treatment of a variety of diseases. The text is as precise, profound as Shang Han Lun, and its topics arguably much more relevant. Our offering is the first systematic teaching of the >Jin Gui Yao Lue in English.

Dr. Sanhu Wang is a medical doctor, chief physician, and professor of medicine at Xi’An TCM hospital, with over four decades of clinical experience. Named as a Renowned TCM Practitioner in both Shaanxi and Guangxi provinces, Dr. Wang is a leading figure in the field for in applying classical texts and formulas in treating modern diseases. Dr. Wang’s lecture series on the Jin Gui Yao Lue was selected among a survey of more than ten difference sources in Chinese, for its accuracy, depth, and the balance between orthodoxy and progressiveness.

It is recommend to study the series as a whole in sequence, but the teachings, categorized by disease type, can also accommodate plug-in-and-use style learning. Familiarity of the Shang Han Lun is helpful but not required for understanding of this text.
