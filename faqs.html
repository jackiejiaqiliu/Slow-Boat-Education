FAQs

Online Courses

Who is eligible to take the online courses?

Our courses are designed for learners at all levels of expertise in TCM. This includes licensed acupuncturists, students in accredited TCM/AOM programs, healthcare professionals interested in TCM, and anyone seeking high-quality NCCAOM-recognized TCM education.

Are the online courses officially accredited?

All of our courses are fully approved by the National Certification Commission for Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine (NCCAOM) as Professional Development Activity (PDA) courses. Each course has an assigned PDA category and credit value. If you are taking our courses for PDA credits, your completed hours will count toward your NCCAOM recertification requirements. PDA credits are also widely accepted for state licensure renewal, but requirements vary by state — please consult your state board for details.

Who are the instructors?

Slow Boat Education partners only with the best TCM practitioners. We maintain a rigorous instructor selection and verification process. All instructors have 10+ years of clinical and/or academic experience in TCM and are widely respected within the field. Instructor bios are available on each course page.

Is there an exam or assessment required?

To ensure learning quality and comply with NCCAOM PDA credit requirements, each course includes a 10-question quiz per PDA credit hour. You may retake quizzes as many times as needed, but you must achieve a minimum score of 70% to proceed to the next session.

What language are the courses offered in?

Courses are offered in either English or Mandarin Chinese, and all include fully bilingual subtitles. All quizzes and related materials are bilingual as well. Recognizing the cultural depth of TCM, we NEVER use AI-generated translations. Every translation is completed manually by experienced TCM practitioners fluent in both languages and cultures, ensuring accuracy and nuance.

Will I receive a certificate of completion?

Yes. If you are taking our course for PDA credits, you will receive an official NCCAOM PDA Certificate of Completion, which may be used toward your PDA requirements for recertification. If you are not taking the course for PDA credit, you will receive a Certificate of Completion issued by Slow Boat Education.

How do I report my PDA credits to NCCAOM?

Slow Boat Education will report PDA credits to NCCAOM on your behalf within 5 business days. When registering for courses, please ensure that your first name, last name, and NCCAOM ID match exactly with your NCCAOM certification record. If you need expedited reporting, please email us at info@slowboatedu.com — we are happy to accommodate when possible.

How long do I have access to the course?

You will have access to the course for one year from your date of registration. All required quizzes must be completed within this one-year timeframe in order to receive your certificate and NCCAOM PDA credits. The course is fully self-paced and can be completed anytime within your access period.

What is your refund policy?

Due to the nature of online learning, we are unable to offer refunds. We encourage you to explore our preview lessons to ensure the course content meets your needs before making a purchase.
